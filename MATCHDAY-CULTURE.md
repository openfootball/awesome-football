# Non-League Match Day Culture — Quick Reference

Concise summary for new non-league fans. Public domain.

## The 3 A's Framework
- **Affordability:** £5–£15 tickets vs £30–£70+ PL
- **Accessibility:** Easy to find, enter, navigate
- **Atmosphere:** Intimate, passionate, authentic

## 7 Golden Tips
1. Start local — find the nearest non-league club
2. Hit the pub first — arrive early, soak in the atmosphere
3. Buy the programme (£2–5) — supports the club
4. Stand on the terrace — open, free, close to the pitch
5. Bring the family — kids run free
6. Try the pie (£3–4) — legendary match-day food
7. Stay after full-time — the clubhouse stays open

## Quick Stats
- Non-League ticket: £5–£15 | PL ticket: £30–£70+ (4–8x more expensive)
- NL entry time: ~20 min | PL entry time: 45+ min
- NL ground capacity: 500–5,000 | PL: 20,000–75,000+
- NL season (20 away): ~£300 | PL: £600–£1,400+
