## Summary of Findings

### What Makes Non-League Match Days Special

1. **Affordability** — Tickets £3–£15 vs £30–£70+ at the Premier League. A full matchday (ticket + food + programme) costs under £20 at Steps 3–7.

2. **Intimacy** — Small grounds (200–5,000 capacity) where you can hear the keeper shouting instructions and feel every tackle. No screens, no VAR, no barriers.

3. **The Tavern & The Clubhouse** — Pre-match pub rituals and clubhouse socialising create a genuine community atmosphere. The social club is where fans, officials, and players interact freely.

4. **The Footy Scran Revolution** — Legendary pies and mash from local bakeries; at Falmouth, Cornish pasties; at Farnham, sweet chilli fried chicken (£7.50); at Lewes, craft beer and locally sourced food.

5. **Physical Programmes** — £2–£3 paper programmes as souvenirs supporting club finances. The digital age hasn't replaced the pleasure of a paper matchday programme.

6. **Terrace Freedom** — No assigned seats, standing anywhere, changing ends at half-time. Football in its purest form.

7. **Volunteer Spirit** — Fans maintain pitches, serve as stewards, sell tickets, distribute programmes, fundraise. The club truly belongs to its community.

8. **Chants & Songs** — Raw, creative, local — not corporate anthems but community songs reflecting generations of support.

9. **Generational Traditions** — Parents bringing children; the kids club as an institution; loyalty passed down.

10. **Away Day Culture** — Traveling the non-league pyramid as a rite of passage; welcoming visiting supporters; the FSA Away Day Experience Awards.

### The 3 A's Framework
- **Affordability** — radical cost advantage over top-flight football
- **Accessibility** — no barriers, direct connection between fans and the club
- **Accountability** — fans know the chairman and manager personally

### Regional Variation
- North: tough, passionate, industrial terrace culture
- Midlands: diverse, community-focused, mix of big and small clubs
- South West/Coastal: relaxed, Cornish pasties, holiday-like atmosphere
- London: diverse, urban, experimental supporter culture
- Lower Steps: hyper-local, intimate, deeply embedded in village life
