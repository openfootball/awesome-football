## Football Culture & Fan Experiences

Non-league football offers some of the most authentic, affordable, and community-driven match day experiences in English football. With tickets at £5-£15 vs £30-£70+ in the Premier League, and grounds seating 500-5,000 fans, the culture is built on proximity, shared experience, and deep connection to local community.

### The 3 A's of Non-League Culture

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Affordability** | Tickets £5-£15 vs £30-£70+ PL. Season cost ~£150-£300 vs £1,500-£3,000+ |
| **Accessibility** | Easy to find, enter, navigate. No membership required. Walk up and play |
| **Accountability** | Fans can approach the chairman or manager directly — no corporate barrier |

### Core Match Day Traditions

| Tradition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| The Pub Signal | Pre-match gatherings at local pubs where fans build community |
| Intimate Grounds | Small stadiums (500-5,000) — supporters are pitchside, not in corporate boxes |
| Freedom of the Terrace | Open standing, no seats, change ends at half-time to follow the ball |
| The Clubhouse | Volunteer-run social club — affordable, family atmosphere, fans mingle with officials |
| Pie & Mash ("Footy Scran") | Iconic match day food — £3-4 pies from local bakeries supporting the club |
| Physical Programme | Printed souvenirs — every penny goes to club finances |
| Volunteer Spirit | Clubs run by volunteers; the "Conference way" ethos |
| Local Rivalries | Deep-rooted geographic derbies with genuine community stakes |
| Family Inclusion | Kids welcome, £5-£15 tickets, safe and nurturing environments |
| Chants & Songs | Organic, locally-written songs reflecting community identity |
| Non-League Day | Annual celebration (March) promoting football below the top four tiers |
| Post-Match Socialising | The clubhouse stays open — the match doesn't end at full-time |

### Fan Sentiment Highlights
