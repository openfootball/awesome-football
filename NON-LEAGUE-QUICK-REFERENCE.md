## Match Day Checklist

- [ ] Buy a physical programme (£2–£4)
- [ ] Try the local food — barmacist, burger van, or club kitchen
- [ ] Visit the clubhouse/social club for a pint
- [ ] Walk the pitch perimeter before kickoff (where allowed)
- [ ] Stand on the terrace — feel the match
- [ ] Talk to a steward or volunteer
- [ ] Log your visit on TheFans.io or Footbeen.com
