# Non-League Match Day Culture — Quick Reference

A concise summary of non-league match day culture and fan experiences for the awesome-football project.

---

## Overview

Non-league football (National League and below) offers an authentic, affordable, and community-driven match day experience that stands in sharp contrast to the commercialised atmosphere of the Premier League.

## The "3 A's"

- **Affordability**: £5–£15 tickets vs £30–£70+ at the Premier League
- **Accessibility**: Walk-on gates, no barriers, 20 minutes before kick-off
- **Accountability**: Club officials know supporters by name; community ownership

## 13 Core Traditions

1. The Pub Signal — Pre-match pub gatherings
2. Intimate Grounds — Small terraced stadiums (500–5,000 capacity)
3. Freedom of the Terrace — Open standing, change ends at half-time
4. The Clubhouse/Social Club — Volunteer-run, family atmosphere
5. Pie, Mash & Gravy — "Footy Scran" from local bakeries
6. Physical Programme — Paper collectables supporting club finances
7. Volunteer Spirit — Community-owned, self-governance
8. Local Rivalries — Deep-rooted geographic derbies
9. Chants & Songs — Organic, locally-written songs
10. Family Inclusion — Kids welcome, no barriers
11. The Conference Legacy — Community ethos from 1979–2004 era
12. Non-League Day — Annual open-doors event
13. Post-Match Socialising — Clubhouse stays open after the final whistle

## Fan Ritual (7-step pattern)

1. Pre-match pint at local social club
2. Buy physical programme (£2–3)
3. Pie, mash & gravy from the clubhouse
4. Stand on terrace inches from the pitch
5. Industrial, locally-written chants
6. Curry/pint after match
7. Go home drained, already anticipating next Saturday
