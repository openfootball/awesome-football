## The 3 A's Framework (Expanded)

### Affordability
- Match tickets: £5–£15 (vs £30–£100+ in the PL)
- Programmes: £2–£3 (vs £5–£10+ in the PL)
- Pint: £2–£4 (vs £5–£7+ in the PL)
- Full away day (ticket + programme + food + drink): ~£25–£35
- **20 away days across the PL ≈ cost of 1 away day at every NL ground**

### Accessibility
- No advance booking required — walk-on gate 20 min before kick-off
- Small grounds (500–5,000 capacity) — you're never far from the pitch
- No long queues, no turnstile queues of 30+ minutes
- Town-centre locations common — pubs and amenities walking distance
- Family-friendly: group bookings, kids on laps, relaxed atmosphere

### Accountability
- Most clubs community-owned or volunteer-run
- Chairman/manager accessible; often speaks to fans directly
- Every penny goes to the club — no shareholder payouts
- Fans steward, serve, and sustain the community
